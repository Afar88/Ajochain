# AjoChain — 2-Minute Tester Guide

Ajo is a simple group savings system where everyone contributes regularly and members take turns receiving the money collected.

You are testing AjoChain on **Chipnet**, a Bitcoin Cash test network. The BCH you use here is **test money and has no real value**.

## 1. Install Selene and switch to Chipnet

1. Install the **Selene wallet** on your phone.
2. Open Selene and create a wallet.
3. In the wallet settings, switch the network to **Chipnet**.
4. If the wallet asks about testnet/Chipnet, choose **Chipnet**.

> **Important:** This test uses Chipnet coins only. Do not use real BCH for this test.

## 2. Get free test BCH

1. Open the Chipnet faucet: https://tbch.googol.cash/
2. Copy your Selene **Chipnet** BCH address.
3. Paste the address into the faucet and request free test BCH.
4. Wait for the BCH to appear in Selene.

## 3. Join the Ajo circle

1. Open the **circle link** the organiser sends you.
2. Tap **Connect Wallet**.
3. Choose **WizardConnect** / **Open in wallet app**.
4. Selene should open and ask you to approve the connection.
5. Approve it and return to AjoChain.

## 4. Contribute your share

1. Check the amount you are expected to contribute.
2. Tap the contribution button.
3. Confirm the transaction in Selene.
4. Wait for AjoChain to show that your contribution was received.

## 5. Watch what happens

Stay on the circle page and watch the status change as members contribute.

When the round is complete, watch for the payout to the member whose turn it is. You may need to wait a little for the Chipnet transaction to confirm.

## 6. Send us your feedback

When you finish testing, send the organiser:

- A screenshot of the **circle status**.
- Anything that confused you, looked wrong, or was difficult to understand.
- If something failed, tell us what you tapped and what happened.

You do **not** need to explain the technical reason for a problem. Just tell us what you saw.

## 🔒 Safety

- **Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.**
- This includes the AjoChain team, organisers, friends, or anyone claiming to provide support.
- AjoChain will never need your seed phrase or private key.
- Only use the Chipnet test coins supplied by the faucet for this test.
